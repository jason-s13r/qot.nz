About Qot. games

Qot. games is a small collection of free games you play in a web browser. They’re made at home in New Zealand by Jason, a software developer, and most of the ideas come from a young game designer in the family who draws them out on paper first.

The games

  • Juice Factory, a clicker game about building a juice empire from one orange.
  • Trick or Treat, a co-op Halloween game on a spooky street.
  • Summer Santa, a co-op Christmas game where Santa delivers to a Kiwi summer.
  • Egg Hunt, a co-op Easter hunt in an autumn garden.
  • Fuse 0xAC, a puzzle game about guarding one number from forged packets.
  • Tessera, a territory game after Empire Attack, played solo against bots or online in games that run for days.

How they’re made

Each game starts as a drawing: houses to visit, things to collect, what’s behind each door. Then it’s built, played at home, argued about, and rebuilt until it’s fun. The games are made to feel like home, too, so you’ll find lollies instead of candy, a dairy on the corner, moreporks, pūkeko, pavlova and gumboots.

They run in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and no accounts.

Keeping them free

The games are free to play. Some show a Google ad, kept away from the buttons you’re tapping, and the seasonal games let a grown-up turn ads off. If you’d like to help keep them going, there’s a Ko-fi page.
