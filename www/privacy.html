Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

The short version

None of the games need an account, an email address or your real name. Tessera lets you pick a name to show other players, and it can be anything you like. Juice Factory and Fuse 0xAC save your progress in your own browser; the seasonal games and Tessera keep it on their server under a random key for your device. Some games show ads from Google, and Google uses cookies to do that.

This site, qot.nz

The pages on qot.nz don’t set cookies or show ads. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches font files from Google.

The games

  • Juice Factory saves your factory in your browser’s local storage on your device. It isn’t sent anywhere. Clearing your browser’s site data, or pressing Reset factory, deletes it.
  • Fuse 0xAC saves the furthest day you’ve reached, your best scores and your sound setting in your browser’s local storage. It isn’t sent anywhere, and clearing the site data for fuse.qot.nz deletes it.
  • Trick or Treat, Summer Santa and Egg Hunt give each device a random key and save progress to the game’s server under that key. There are no names or emails, rooms are private, there’s no chat, and player names come from each game’s own list. A grown-up can move, back up or delete a profile in the game’s Keep safe area.
  • Tessera has no accounts and sets no cookies. Your browser keeps a key that identifies you in games, and the name you choose. Online games (each player’s key and name, the moves and the chat) are kept on the game’s server so they can run while everyone’s away, and are deleted 30 days after the game ends. Tessera shows no ads.

Ads

Games that show ads use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • Juice Factory shows one ad at the bottom of the page, and Fuse 0xAC may show ads too. Depending on your choices, these can be based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Trick or Treat, Summer Santa and Egg Hunt may show an ad between rounds. These are marked as for a children’s site, so they aren’t personalised, and a grown-up can turn them off in Keep safe.
  • How Google uses information from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Turn personalised ads off, or manage them: myadcenter.google.com
  • Opt out of other ad networks’ personalised ads: aboutads.info/choices

If you’re in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before using cookies for personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

Hosting and tips

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the games, and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect them.
  • Ko-fi handles tips if you choose to leave one. That happens on Ko-fi’s own site, under its privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
