Privacy

Fuse 0xAC is a free browser game from Qot. games. There are no accounts, and the game does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Last updated 30 October 2026.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your own device using your browser's local storage: the furthest day you've reached, your best scores and whether sound is muted. This data stays in your browser and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data for fuse.qot.nz removes it.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising

Qot. games may show ads from Google AdSense to help cover running costs. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads, and may use them to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and read more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required, you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Fonts and other requests

Fonts and all game files are served from this site. The game itself makes no other third-party requests.

Tips

The Ko-fi link takes you to Ko-fi's own site, which has its own privacy policy. We only see what Ko-fi shares with creators about tips you choose to send.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
